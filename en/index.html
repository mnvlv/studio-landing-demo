Our approach

We build websites and AI tools that work for the business, not just look good

We start with the goal: more leads, less manual work, faster replies to customers. Then we choose the solution and take it all the way to launch.

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    We analyse the task

    We have a call to clarify goals, audience and constraints, and agree on what will count as the result.

  2. 02

    We propose a solution

    We prepare the scope, timeline and estimate, and explain why we chose this particular stack.

  3. 03

    We build

    Design, markup, logic and integrations. We show interim versions so you can see the progress.

  4. 04

    We test

    We check different devices and browsers, loading speed, forms and user scenarios.

  5. 05

    We launch and support

    We publish, connect analytics, hand over access and, if needed, stay on for support.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope: number of pages, design, integrations and features. You can get a rough figure in the calculator; we prepare an exact estimate after discussing the task.

How long does development take?

The timeline depends on the scope and is fixed in the estimate before we start. Simple projects are faster; projects with integrations and AI take longer.

What do you need from us to get started?

A description of the task, examples of websites you like and materials about the company: texts, photos, logo. If something is missing, we will help prepare it.

Who will own the website and the code?

You will. After payment we hand over the source code and access to hosting, the domain and connected services.

What is an AI agent and why does a business need one?

It is a program based on a neural network that performs a task: answers customers, sorts requests, finds information in your documents. It takes repetitive work off your staff.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. First we look at how it is built and tell you honestly which is more cost-effective: improving it or building a new one.

Do you support the website after launch?

Yes, we can stay on for support: updates, improvements, monitoring and backups.
